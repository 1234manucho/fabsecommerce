Buyer FAQ

Everything buyers need before they inquire.

Clear guidance on catalogue scale, MOQ, product media, custom manufacturing, shipping and payment.

Visit buyer support →
01How large is the FABS games catalogue?+

The marketplace is structured for more than 2.3 million searchable listings across up to 999 catalogue pages, covering arcade, VR, tabletop, racing and venue equipment categories.

02What does MOQ mean on a product listing?+

MOQ is the minimum order quantity accepted for a listing. Commercial machines may begin at one unit, while custom cards, boards and branded products may require hundreds or thousands of pieces.

03How do I request a supplier price?+

Open a product and choose Send Inquiry. Enter quantity, destination and required specifications. The system stores the request and returns a traceable FABS inquiry reference.

04Can FABS source customised or OEM games?+

Yes. Buyers can request custom artwork, cabinet finishes, packaging, components, language versions and OEM production, subject to sample and compliance approval.

05Are the product photos real?+

Yes. The featured homepage and catalogue media are self-hosted real venue, supplier and manufacturer product photographs, with the source presented on each listing.

06How does shipping work?+

We ship worldwide via DHL Express. Live shipping rates appear on every product page and at checkout. Duties and taxes are estimated at checkout for international buyers, and every shipment includes a tracking number.

07Which payment methods do you accept?+

Card payments are processed by DPO · 3G Direct Pay on a PCI-DSS Level 1 hosted page. We accept Visa and Mastercard in USD and KES. FABS Trading never sees or stores your card number or security code.

08Is my card data safe with FABS?+

Yes. Card details are entered directly on DPO's secure hosted checkout page — they never touch FABS servers. Every payment runs through 3-D Secure bank verification and returns a signed transaction reference we can reconcile against your order.
