### Required RFQ information

1. Contracting buyer and authorised contact
2. Product, grade, standard and intended application
3. Quantity, packaging and delivery window
4. Destination port or delivery point and preferred incoterm
5. Required technical, quality, origin, safety and shipping documents
6. Settlement expectations and any inspection requirement

## Digital catalogue standard

The launch catalogue contains 12 fictional presentation titles using real gaming-environment photography. Before any commercial launch, replace demonstration titles with products supplied through authorised publisher or distributor agreements.

Each product record documents:

- Title, genre, platform and edition
- Supported region and activation channel
- Current and comparison price
- Expected delivery timing
- Included features and base-game dependencies
- Order, payment and fulfilment references
- Approved support and refund route

### Marketplace interaction standard

- Cover artwork remains visible as a reliable poster fallback.
- Mouse hover, keyboard focus or touch starts a muted, looping video preview.
- A second touch pauses the preview; pointer exit resets it on desktop.
- “View deal” is deliberately separate from the preview control and opens the full product record.
- Purchased titles run through their supported publisher platform after activation; catalogue previews are presentation media, not embedded game executables.
- Remote video is loaded lazily in the Flask build to reduce initial catalogue bandwidth.

## Media governance

Remote media provides a board-ready presentation layer. Before production, the content owner should approve licensing, captions, long-term availability, accessibility and relevance. Approved images and videos may be transferred to an organisation-controlled CDN without changing the product-data model. Replace presentation titles and footage with authorised publisher assets before commercial release.
