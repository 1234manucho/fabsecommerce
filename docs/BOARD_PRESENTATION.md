# FABS Trading — Board System Brief

## Executive proposition

FABS Trading is a dual-market commerce company with a shared technology, finance, control and customer-service foundation. The customer-facing product is intentionally separated into:

1. **Digital games** — high-volume retail discovery, secure card checkout and digital fulfilment.
2. **Industrial commodities** — specification-led B2B origination, KYB, commercial offers, contracts, settlement and logistics.

## Why the separation matters

The two divisions have different risk, margin, fulfilment and customer-support requirements. They share identity, security, reporting and governance, but not a single simplistic checkout workflow.

| Area | Games | Commodities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | Product catalogue | Non-binding RFQ |
| Primary control | Platform/region and fraud | KYB, sanctions, specification, contract |
| Payment | Bank-hosted card checkout | Contract-approved route; MID only where approved |
| Fulfilment | Digital entitlement | Inspection, documentation and logistics |
| Evidence | Order and provider references | Offer, contract, shipping and inspection records |

## Operating dashboards

- Orders, payment state and reconciliation
- Digital fulfilment status
- RFQ pipeline and assigned trading-desk responsibility
- Product catalogue and availability
- Customer and business-account records
- Webhook events and administrative audit trail

## Customer-facing information architecture

The board presentation experience now includes specialist page groups for digital commerce, industrial procurement, company governance and market education. High-impact live images and muted background video are used to establish context, while product, transaction and policy content stays accessible as HTML.

- Digital: catalogue, product detail, delivery, corporate sales, cart, checkout and support
- Industrial: portfolio, product detail, industries, logistics, quality and RFQ
- Company: about, sustainability, partnerships, investors and careers
- Resources: newsroom articles, compliance, contact and legal policies

The presentation layer now opens with an animated global-commerce visual using real Earth imagery. Moving market cards connect the two operating divisions to logistics, while catalogue rails expose 12 digital products and all 18 industrial lines before the detailed catalogue controls.

Media URLs are presentation assets and must be reviewed for long-term licensing, uptime and brand suitability before final production launch. Production teams may self-host approved assets without changing the template structure.

## Board decisions required before production

- Contracting entity, target markets and legal policies
- Game publishers/distributors and licence provenance
- Commodity supplier, inspection and logistics framework
- Acquiring bank, approved MCC/MID use cases, currencies and limits
- KYB, sanctions, fraud and transaction-monitoring providers
- Support service levels, refund authority and incident ownership
- Hosting region, retention schedule and business-continuity plan
- Final media licensing, asset hosting and content-approval ownership
